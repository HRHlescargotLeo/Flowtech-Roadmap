Prototype 2 of 5 · Product page · ideas 9–18

What we're proposing

A product page that answers the three things a buyer needs before ordering: when it will arrive (a delivery date for their postcode and quantity, before the basket), what it costs (with or without VAT, per pack and per unit) and what else they need (tube, cutter and plugs that fit). Stock at the buyer's own branch sits beside national stock. Specs show only fields with values, and look-alike products explain how they differ.

Why

Today a non-stock item says "Please add desired quantity to your basket to see any applicable lead times". The FT Pro 8mm tee's specification shows "0�C to +60�C", its variant table showed one set of prices and then another while loading, and the Top Selling carousel lists two products with the same name at £1.15 and £6.48 with nothing to say why. The SMC union tee KQ2T08-00A is listed as "MALE FITTING, D 8 TUBE" under Male Studs, with 13 empty variant columns. None of these is hard to fix, and between them they decide whether a buyer trusts the page enough to order from it.

Open any product from the listing, or try KQ2T08-00A (not in stock) and FT-PU08-30B (sold by the reel).
