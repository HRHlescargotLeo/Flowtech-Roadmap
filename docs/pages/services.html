Prototype 4 of 5 · Engineering services · ideas 8 and 24–30

What we're proposing

Give the engineering side of Flowtech the same structure as the product side: a page for each service, a page for each sector, a case study library that can be filtered, and two tools that turn interest into work (a hose assembly builder and online booking of a service visit). Every service page lists the sites that offer it, which links this prototype to the branch finder.

Why

Services is currently one page of headings and lists: seven services and six capabilities with no links, seven industries with no pages, and a page for export that exists but isn't linked from it. Projects is headed "Case studies" and links to none; /case-studies is a 404. The FY25 results name engineering projects as one of four growth levers and data centres, defence, and bridges and waterways as target sectors. The website doesn't yet reflect any of that.
