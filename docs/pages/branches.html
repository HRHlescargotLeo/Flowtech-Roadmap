What we're proposing

A branch finder for all 26 sites across the UK, Ireland and the Benelux, including the Thorite, Allswage and Thomas Group branches: search by postcode or town, filter by what each site does (hose assembly, repairs, click and collect, training), see who's open now, and set a site as "my branch". That choice then follows the buyer around the site, showing local stock on product pages and routing service enquiries.

Why

The network is one of Flowtech's real advantages and the website barely shows it. Contact lists 25 locations in an accordion with no map or search, and leaves out Leicester, which the Locations page includes. Collection is described as "from our distribution centre", while Thorite's own site already offers click and collect from its branches. With five acquisitions in 18 months, a single finder is also the simplest way to show the group as one business.

Towns and group brands follow the live Contact and Locations pages. Only County Durham's address and phone are real; capabilities, hours and the map positions are samples.
