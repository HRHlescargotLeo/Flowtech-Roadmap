What we're proposing

Quotes live in the account, listed with their status, value and expiry date. A ready quote opens to show its lines and goes into the basket at the quoted prices in one click. Expired quotes offer a re-quote rather than a dead end.

Why

The homepage currently gives its second slot, above the welcome heading, to a "Retrieving A Quote?" panel that only works once you're logged in and know your reference number. That space is better spent on finding products, and the quote belongs where the buyer already is when they come back to order.

Shown as a signed-in trade account. Quote prices are sample figures (catalogue price less 8%).
