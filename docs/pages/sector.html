What we're proposing

A sector page brings together everything Flowtech does for one industry: the problems it solves, the services and product ranges that apply, case studies and recent news. Shown here with data centres. Bridges, waterways and flood defence is the most complete, because Flowtech already has named projects to point at.

Why

Flowtech exhibited at Data Centre World London 2026 and names data centres as a target sector, but a data centre engineer arriving at the site finds no page about their world. The same goes for defence and for bridges, where Tower Bridge, Narrow Water Bridge and Rice Bridge are mentioned only in news stories and a sentence on Projects.
