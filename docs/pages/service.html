What we're proposing

One template for every engineering service: what's included, typical turnaround, the sites that offer it, a case study and a short enquiry form that already knows which service it's about. Where there's a tool (book a visit, build a hose), it sits at the top.

Why

A buyer searching for "hydraulic cylinder repair" has nowhere specific to land today, only a line in a list on the Services page. A page per service gives search engines and buyers something to find, and gives the sales team a link to send.

Switch service with the links at the bottom of the page. Turnaround times and some inclusions are samples, marked [sample].
