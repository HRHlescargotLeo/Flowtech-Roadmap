What we're proposing

One place for every project Flowtech can talk about, filterable by sector and region, each written the same way: challenge, solution, result, and the service behind it. Sector and service pages pull from the same library, so a case study is written once and appears wherever it's relevant.

Why

/case-studies returns a 404. Halley VI has a good case study page, but the only link to it we found is a homepage carousel slide. Projects mentions Tower Bridge and Halley VI in a sentence and links to neither, and the Knowledge Hub's "Case Studies" link takes you back to Projects. Flowtech has better stories than most of its competitors and is hiding them.

Halley VI is summarised from the live case study. The others are real projects from Flowtech's news, with details left as [placeholders].
