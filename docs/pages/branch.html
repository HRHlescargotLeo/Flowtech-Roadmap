What we're proposing

One template for every site: how to reach it, when it's open, what it does, how click and collect works there, a stock check for that branch, nearby sites and, where the site runs them, training courses that can be booked online. Group brands get the same page with a line explaining they're part of Flowtech.

Why

Each site has a few lines in an accordion today. Gloucester hosted a two-day BFPA Hose Assembly Skills Training programme, which appears only as a news story, and Thorite's site already sells training places. A branch page is also where local search sends people looking for "hydraulic hose repair near me".

Shown with Gloucester. Open County Durham to see a site with its real address and phone number.
