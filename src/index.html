Five ideas for flowtech.co.uk

Clickable prototypes of five improvements to the Flowtech website, from finding the right fitting to booking an engineer. Each one works as it would on the live site. They are greyscale on purpose, so the conversation stays on what they do rather than how they look.

Version 1 1 October 2026 Greyscale prototypes Sample data

The prototypes

  1. Find a fitting
    1

    Find the right fitting fast

    A push-in fittings listing that leads with the filters buyers use, shows availability with counts and offers a compact list view, plus a step-by-step fitting finder and equivalents for other makers' codes.

    Try: filter to 8mm tees in stock, switch to list view, then search within the category for QST-8.

    Open prototype
  2. Product page
    2

    A product page that answers when, how much and what fits

    A delivery date for your postcode before the basket, prices with or without VAT, stock at your branch, the tube and tools that go with it, clean specifications and an explanation when two products share a name.

    Try: enter 1,000 as the quantity and check the date for GL2 5JY, then switch prices to inc VAT.

    Open prototype
  3. Order and quote
    3

    Order and quote like a trade buyer

    Quick order with paste from Excel or CSV upload, a quote request that takes a parts list and drawings, My quotes in the account, and a shorter, properly labelled registration.

    Try: fill the paste box with the example and add the lines, then open quote Q-48213 and add it to the basket.

    Open prototype
  4. Engineering services
    4

    Engineering services that sell themselves

    A page for each service and sector, a case study library, a hose assembly builder and online booking of a service visit or workshop repair.

    Try: build a 2SN hose with 90° ends at both sides, then book a workshop repair for postcode WF8 1AA.

    Open prototype
  5. Find a branch
    5

    Find your nearest Flowtech

    A branch finder for every site in the group, with postcode search, a map, capability filters, opening hours and "my branch", which then shows local stock on product pages. Each site gets a branch page with click and collect and training.

    Try: search for Gloucester, set it as your branch, then go back to the product page and look at the stock panel.

    Open prototype

How to review

  • Everything interactive works: filters, the finder, delivery dates, quick order, quote and booking forms, the hose builder, the branch map. Links to pages outside these five go nowhere by design.
  • Use the bar at the top to move between prototypes. Switch Notes on (top right) to see what each prototype proposes and why, plus notes on behaviour (yellow) and open questions for Flowtech (red). Notes start switched off.
  • The basket, your VAT choice and "my branch" carry between prototypes for the session, so the pieces can be tried together.
  • Product codes, names, prices and national stock follow the live site where we saw them. Branch stock, lead times, quotes, hose prices, course dates and anything in [brackets] are samples.
  • Works on a phone: open this page on your mobile to check the layouts.
