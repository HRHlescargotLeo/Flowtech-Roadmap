What we're proposing

Four pages for trade buyers who already know what they want. Quick order takes codes and quantities typed, pasted from Excel or uploaded as a CSV, and shows the product, stock and price as each code goes in. Request a quote takes a parts list and drawings. My quotes lists every quote with its status and turns a ready quote into an order. Registration is shorter and properly labelled.

Why

"Quick Order" in the live header opens the basket, and the Quick Add page explains that CSV upload lives on the basket page, so the feature exists but takes some finding. The contact form is the only way to ask for a quote: five fields, no parts list, no attachments. Quote retrieval sits on the homepage above the H1 and needs a login first. Repeat buyers are probably most of the revenue, so this is where small changes pay back fastest.
