What we're proposing

A quote request that takes what a buyer actually has to hand: a parts list (pasted or uploaded), drawings or photos, a delivery postcode and a date. Each line is matched against the catalogue as it is pasted, and lines we can't match are accepted rather than rejected, because identifying them is part of the service.

Why

The only route today is the contact form, with a "What can we help you with?" menu, name, company, email and one message box. A buyer with a 40-line bill of materials has to type it out or email it separately, and the sales team then has to re-key it. The request arrives with no phone number and no account number, which presumably costs a call before any work starts.
