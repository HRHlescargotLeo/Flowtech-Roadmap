What we're proposing

Specify a hydraulic hose assembly online: hose type, bore, overall length, the fitting at each end, orientation if both ends are bent, a sleeve and a pressure test. The page shows the working pressure, a price and where and when it will be made, then puts it in the basket or turns it into a quote request.

Why

Bespoke hose assemblies are the first service listed under Bespoke Product Manufacture, and the FAQ asks "Can I check lead times on assembly products?", but there is no way to specify one online. Competitors who sell hose online lead with a builder. It is the one engineering service that behaves like a product, so it's the natural bridge between the two halves of the business.

Hose types, end fittings, pressures and prices are samples. The real options and price rules would come from Flowtech's hose ranges.
