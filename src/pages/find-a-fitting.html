What we're proposing

A category listing that leads with the five or six filters a buyer actually uses for push-in fittings (tube size, connection, shape, thread and brand), with counts beside each option, an availability filter and the rest tucked behind More filters. Alongside it, a compact list view for people who buy by code and a step-by-step fitting finder for people who don't.

Why

The live Push-in Fittings & Tubing listing has 13,159 products, shows 15 at a time and offers about 78 filters in alphabetical order, so Accessories and Actuation come before tube size, and Gauge Diameter and Number of Stations appear on a page of fittings. Availability is a single on/off switch. Most of this is configuration in the platform rather than new build, which helps.

The prototype uses 28 sample fittings. Codes, names, prices and national stock follow the live site where we saw them.
