What we're proposing

The same registration, rebuilt so it works for everyone: every field has a visible label tied to it, hints sit under the label rather than in the placeholder, the company can be found by Companies House number, and the password rules are shown before you start typing rather than after you fail them.

Why

The live form has 14 inputs and none of them has a programmatically associated label, so a screen reader announces most of them as an unnamed text box. The account number hint is in the placeholder ("e.g. If you already have a Flowtech Account") and disappears as soon as you type. It is the front door for every new online customer, and a modest piece of work.
