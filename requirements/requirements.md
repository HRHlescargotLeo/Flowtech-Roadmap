# Flowtech — prototype requirements (V1)

Source: ClerksWell phase 1 review of flowtech.co.uk and five competitors (1 Oct 2026).
Idea numbers (#nn) refer to the Flowtech Website Opportunities page. These are ClerksWell
proposals, not client requirements; Flowtech has not yet reviewed them.

## Global
- R01 A prototype navigator replaces Flowtech's header: Overview, prototypes 1–5, a sample basket count and a Notes switch (off by default).
- R02 Every template works at 320px, 768px and 1024px+, with no horizontal scroll.
- R03 All interactive components are keyboard operable and dismissible with Escape; visible 3px focus outline (WCAG 2.2 AA target) (#45–#47).
- R04 The basket, VAT choice and "my branch" persist across pages for the session.

## Prototype 1 — Find a fitting (ideas #1–#7, #52)
- R10 Category listing leads with the filters that fit the category (tube OD, connection, shape, thread, brand); the rest sit in a More filters drawer (#1).
- R11 Availability filter with counts: any, in stock now, available to order (#2).
- R12 Grid and compact list views; 12/24/48 per page; sort by relevance, price and stock; choices remembered (#3).
- R13 Product cards show brand, code, key specs as chips, price with VAT state, stock and quantity + Add (#3, #52).
- R14 A step-by-step fitting finder (tube size → connection → thread → shape) with result counts per option and a link to the filtered listing (#6).
- R15 Another maker's part number returns equivalents from the catalogue, FT Pro first, clearly labelled as equivalents (#7).
- R16 Search within the category; active filters shown as removable chips; filters never lead to an empty page (#1).

## Prototype 2 — Product page (ideas #9–#18)
- R20 Price shown exc or inc VAT, with the other figure beside it and a line total for the quantity (#14).
- R21 VAT choice is a toggle remembered across pages (#14).
- R22 Delivery date by postcode and quantity before the basket, using the 10pm cut-off, excluded postcode areas and lead times for shortfalls (#13).
- R23 National stock plus stock at the buyer's branch, with collection time (#15).
- R24 Specifications show only populated fields; description to house standard; no broken characters (#9, #12, #17).
- R25 Other sizes in the range as a table with stock and Add (#12).
- R26 "You may also need": compatible tube, tools and accessories, multi-add (#16).
- R27 Equivalents from other brands (#7).
- R28 Products that share a name explain how they differ (#11).
- R29 Downloads: tech sheet, CAD and certificates where available (#18).

## Prototype 3 — Order and quote (ideas #19–#23)
- R30 Quick order rows: code and quantity with live product, stock and price lookup; unknown and other makers' codes flagged (#19).
- R31 Paste lines from Excel or upload a CSV (#19).
- R32 Basket with editable quantities, carriage threshold and totals exc/inc VAT (#19).
- R33 Quote request with parts list (paste or file), postcode, date needed, phone and account number; unmatched lines accepted (#20).
- R34 Drawings and photos can be attached, with a size limit and clear error (#20).
- R35 My quotes in the account: search by number, filter by status, view lines, add a ready quote to the basket at quoted prices (#21).
- R36 Registration with associated labels, hints under labels, Companies House lookup and visible password rules (#22).

## Prototype 4 — Engineering services (ideas #8, #24–#30, #42)
- R40 Services hub: a card per service, sector links (target sectors marked) and featured case studies (#24, #25).
- R41 Service template: what's included, turnaround, sites offering it, case study, related services (#24).
- R42 Sector template: needs, services, product ranges, case studies, news (#25).
- R43 Case study library filterable by sector and region; one structure (challenge, solution, result) (#26).
- R44 Hose assembly builder: type, bore, length, ends, orientation, options; pressure, price and lead time; add to basket or request a quote (#8).
- R45 Book a service visit in four steps, nearest capable centres by postcode, emergency route with out-of-hours charge (#27).
- R46 Short enquiry forms on service pages and a project brief form that routes to the right site (#28).
- R47 Named customer quotes, as placeholders until Flowtech supplies approved ones (#42).

## Prototype 5 — Find your nearest Flowtech (ideas #15, #31, #32, #34–#36)
- R50 Branch search by postcode or town, sorted by distance (#31).
- R51 Map with a pin per site; selecting a pin highlights its branch (#31).
- R52 Filter by capability (hose assembly, repair, testing, click & collect, training and others) (#31).
- R53 "Set as my branch" carries through to product stock, hose builder and service enquiries (#15).
- R54 Opening hours with an "open now" state and filter (#31).
- R55 Group brands (Thorite, Allswage, Thomas Group) shown as part of Flowtech (#34).
- R56 Branch template: contact, hours, capabilities, services, nearby sites (#31).
- R57 Click and collect explained per site, with a stock check for that branch (#32).
- R58 Training courses listed and bookable where the site runs them (#36).
